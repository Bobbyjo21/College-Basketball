How the realistic model works

Possession by possession
1Estimate possessions from adjusted tempo
2Match shooting and offensive efficiency against the defense
3Apply rest, travel, home court and player availability
4Simulate twos, threes, free throws, turnovers and overtime

Correlated measurements are grouped before weighting to reduce double-counting. Measured data overrides the rating-based prior; missing fields fall back safely instead of becoming zero.

Core matchup weighting: overall efficiency 30%, shooting quality 18%, turnovers 12%, rebounding 10%, ratings/opponent strength 15%, free throws and fouls 5%, pace 5%, and recent/situational performance 5%. Direct point adjustments for home court, rest, travel and availability are applied afterward.
